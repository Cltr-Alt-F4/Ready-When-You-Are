Game Idea 1 

<u>Title :” Ready When You Are”</u> 

Description : 2D pixelated game with 2 phases and 2 players . 

Style : Asymmetric Horror Requirements : Has to be a web game that can be play on Mobile device and PC Dev time : 1 months 

# <u>Game Play Description</u> 

# <u>First Phase</u> 

2 Players with their own map, which has hiding places for objects . 

Both of them has to hide them in 1:30 time mark , 3 items . 

Times up , each player transported to their opponent map , has to look for the items . time limit 2 minutes . 

Who ever found 3 objects win first phase. 

# <u>Second Phase</u> 

After the first phase and we got the winner , both players transported to a new map , with 3 shrines randomly spawned . The winner become the hider who has to placed the objects he found in the 3 randomly placed shrines to win . The loser in first round become the seeker , and has to chase and catch hider to win . 

# <u>Game Mechanic</u> 

# <u>First Phase</u> 

10 or 15 predefined hiding places on map , player can choose freely to place them . Game start , they have a shovel icon button that has 3 seconds cooldown , they can dig up 

place to look for them . 

# <u>Second Phase</u> 

The Map Will be Night with raining constantly 

Hider : Has a flashlight that can view an angle of 3 tiles , can run with a cooldown  , 

Seeker , cannot see , but the lighting strike every 5 sec and you can see the hider place for 2 or 3 secs . and also has echo location ability with cooldown , that can sense hider location . 

Speed boost for both player during lighting strike , but the seeker got a higher boost . 

Add on : 

The three objects you have to hide will be randomized by algorithm and the hider has to use those during hunt , 

Flashlight as a item cannot be removed , 

Rubber Shoes, which will let seeker hear the shoes sounds Snowball, to throw at seekers to slowdown Alarm , that can go off at any time that will give hider position Dummy toy , that can give misinformation to seeker . 

Tech stacks : HTML , CSS , JS 

Arts & Designs : itch.io , nanobanana 

Version Control : Git , GitHub 

Software & Resources : VScode , Phaser 3 , Godot 

Challenge : As a webgame , the  2 players need to be in sync , connected through Websockets
